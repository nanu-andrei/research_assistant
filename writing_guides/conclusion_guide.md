# Academic Writing Guide: Conclusion & Future Work Chapter (IEEE Standard)

## Objective
The Conclusion chapter summarizes key findings, critically outlines known limitations, and provides concrete, actionable future research directions.

## Industry Standard Structure
1. **Executive Synthesis (¶1):**
   - High-level recapitulation of the problem, proposed system architecture, and main empirical breakthroughs.
2. **Explicit Discussion of Limitations (¶2):**
   - Honest, scientific transparency regarding boundary conditions (e.g. multi-occupant interference, extreme non-line-of-sight attenuation, battery constraints).
3. **Actionable Future Research Directions (¶3):**
   - Concrete roadmaps: Multi-modal RF-vision fusion (Wi2Vi), federated privacy-preserving learning, and neuromorphic edge hardware.
