# Academic Writing Guide: Related Work Chapter (IEEE Standard)

## Objective
The Related Work chapter must not be a mere chronological bibliography list. It should be a structured, critical synthesis that organizes prior research into clear conceptual taxonomies and demonstrates where the current work fits into the scientific landscape.

## Industry Standard Structure
1. **Thematic Subsection Breakdown:**
   - Divide into 2-3 focused thematic areas (e.g., *A. Device-Free WiFi Activity Sensing*, *B. Deep Learning on RF/CSI Data*, *C. Cross-Domain Generalization*).
2. **Thematic Synthesis vs. Individual Summaries:**
   - Group related papers together: *"Several early works [1, 2] explored RSSI heuristics, but modern pipelines [3, 4] universally adopt physical-layer Channel State Information (CSI)..."*
3. **Contrastive Positioning:**
   - Every subsection must conclude with a sharp differentiator: *"Unlike [X], which requires per-room offline calibration, our proposed framework..."*
4. **Summary Comparison Table:**
   - Provide a LaTeX comparative matrix contrasting Sensing Modality, Architecture, Target Accuracy, and Environmental Invariance.

## Weighting Rules
- Top-ranked papers from Phase 2 Related Work form the core taxonomy categories.
- Their specific architectural strengths and vulnerabilities are explicitly highlighted.
